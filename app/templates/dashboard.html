ABOUT EASYAPPLY

Your job search, step by step.

EasyApply brings resume preparation, job discovery, and application tracking into one workspace. You stay in control of which opportunities move forward.

  1. 01

    Set up your profile

    Add your experience, preferred roles, and locations so recommendations have useful context.

    Complete your profile ↗
  2. 02

    Upload your resumes

    Upload PDF or DOCX versions. EasyApply extracts and analyzes resume text to help match openings.

    Manage resumes ↗
  3. 03

    Discover and review jobs

    Run the AI Job Agent to see recommendations and the selected resume. Open Application visits the employer listing without saving an application.

    Explore jobs ↗
  4. 04

    AutoApply and track

    AutoApply saves the job and selected resume, then attempts supported submission. “Saved — not confirmed” means the job is recorded; “Applied” means submission was confirmed.

    View applications ↗
Good to know

Job recommendations are temporary until you choose AutoApply. Automated submission is supported only for compatible application flows; EasyApply does not guess answers to custom questions.
